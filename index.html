Security & access

This browser uses an encrypted local vault.

On iPhone, device unlock may use Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. Vidya never receives your biometric data.

Backup & install

Download an encrypted backup before clearing browser data or changing devices. The backup requires the vault password that existed when it was created.

Privacy

Tasks, chats, memories, settings and extracted document text are encrypted at rest in this browser. This is a device-local vault—not a cross-device account. For confidential employer material, also use a permanent HTTPS domain you control, protect the hosting account, keep AI keys server-side, and confirm your employer permits the upload.

The Canadian softener

At work
“I might be missing something—could we look at the assumption behind that timeline?”
What it signals

Curiosity and respect while still challenging the idea.

Hidden norm

Disagreement is often softened linguistically, especially in mixed-seniority groups.

Do not confuse it with

Lack of conviction. The wording protects the relationship, not the quality bar.
